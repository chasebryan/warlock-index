  captured.
- Treat current reporting as a lead layer, not as implementation proof.
- Do not upgrade sanctions, legal, nuclear, UN, maritime, defense posture,
  cyber, or operational claims without primary source records.
- Keep live and map sources out of operational use.
- Use separate product lanes for official source capture, research synthesis,
  current-event reporting, and implementation watches.
